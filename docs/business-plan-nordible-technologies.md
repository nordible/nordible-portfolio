## 1. Executive Summary (*Geschäftsidee & Unternehmensprofil*)

### 1.1 The Business Concept: The AI-Native Agency Model
Nordible Technologies is an **AI-Native Engineering & Growth Agency** based in Frankfurt am Main. Operating under the high-margin **"Services as Software"** paradigm, Nordible breaks the traditional linear agency model (where increasing revenue requires hiring more billable junior staff). Instead, Nordible pairs a 15-year veteran principal systems architect with specialized autonomous AI agent pipelines.

By automating repetitive engineering workflows (scaffolding, schema generation, automated unit/integration testing, CI/CD orchestration, and GEO content operations), Nordible delivers the output velocity and breadth of a 10-person agency at **70–80% gross margins**, with zero architectural debt and 100% intellectual property ownership transferred to the client.

### 1.2 Core Productized Service Packages
Rather than selling ambiguous billable hours, Nordible sells **guaranteed outcomes** through three standardized tiers:

1. **AI-Accelerated MVP Build (€14,500 fixed fee | 4-week delivery):**  
   Converting validated enterprise and startup specifications into production-grade web & mobile applications with automated test coverage, database schema migrations, and AWS cloud deployment.
2. **Agentic Cloud & Systems Architecture Retainer (€6,500 / month):**  
   Principal-level infrastructure modernization, self-healing microservices, automated API maintenance, and FDA/HIPAA/PCI-DSS regulatory compliance pipelines.
3. **AI Digital Growth & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Retainer (€3,500 / month):**  
   Multi-channel marketing automation, social media operations, and technical optimization for AI answer engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Google SGE) to drive qualified B2B client acquisition.
4. **Full-Stack Growth Retainer (€9,500 / month):**  
   Combined engineering leadership and digital marketing management for scaling scale-ups and SMEs.

### 1.3 Regional Economic Impact for Frankfurt & Hessen
- **Alleviating the Severe IT Shortage (*Fachkräftemangel*):** Providing Frankfurt startups and German *Mittelstand* enterprises with immediate, high-velocity technical capability without long 6-month recruitment delays.
- **Fiscal Contribution:** Generating local trade tax (*Gewerbesteuer*), income tax (*Einkommensteuer*), and contributing to the German social/private healthcare infrastructure.
- **Strengthening Frankfurt as an AI Innovation Center:** Demonstrating cutting-edge AI-native workflow efficiency within the Frankfurt Rhine-Main metropolitan area.
